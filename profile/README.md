# e123

We build software that powers the modern insurance experience. From quoting and enrollment to servicing, payments, and analytics. Our platform helps carriers, agencies, and partners deliver better outcomes for the people they serve.

## What we do

- **Distribution & enrollment**: Tools that streamline the path from shopper to active policyholder.
- **Servicing & operations**: Systems that keep the business running once the policy is in force.
- **Data & insights**: A unified view of the book, the funnel, and the customer.
- **Integrations**: APIs and connectors that meet partners where they already work.

## Engineering at e123

We care about clean architecture, pragmatic delivery, and the craft of building software that holds up under real-world load. Our stack spans modern TypeScript, React, Node, ColdFusion, and a mix of relational and cloud-native data services, deployed on AWS.
